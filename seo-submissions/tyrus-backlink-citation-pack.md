Title: Why Bulk Document Scanning Needs OCR and Indexing

Summary: Bulk scanning becomes useful only when records are indexed properly and delivered in searchable formats.

Article:

Bulk document scanning is not just about scanning pages quickly. Large record rooms often contain cartons, files, old registers, invoices, KYC records, HR documents and legal files. If these records are scanned without indexing, teams may still struggle to find the right document later.

OCR and metadata indexing make bulk scanning useful. Common fields include customer ID, MRN, invoice number, file number, department, branch, date, vendor and record type. These fields help teams retrieve records quickly from a DMS, ERP, EMR, cloud archive or secure folder system.
