Document archival services are different from ordinary scanning. Archive projects must preserve retrieval context, record history, box references, file sequence and long-term usability.

Many organizations have years of inactive records stored in cartons, cupboards, branches or external storage. Digitizing those records reduces retrieval time and protects documents from repeated handling.

## What archive digitization includes

A practical archive digitization project includes:

- Inventory of boxes and record types
- Folder and naming standards
- Scanning and image quality checks
- OCR and searchable PDF or PDF/A
- Metadata capture
- Box-to-digital cross-reference sheets
- Exception reports for missing or damaged records
- Secure digital archive handover

## Common archive records

Archive digitization often covers:

- Government records and registers
- Medical records and MRD files
- Finance files and invoices
- KYC and loan records
- Legal case files and contracts
- HR employee files
- Old office records
- Bound registers and fragile documents

## Final thought

Archive scanning should be planned around how people will retrieve records later. A good archive is not just scanned images; it is a searchable, structured, traceable record system.
