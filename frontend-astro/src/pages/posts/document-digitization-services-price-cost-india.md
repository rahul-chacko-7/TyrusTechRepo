**Professional document scanning in India typically costs ₹1.20 to ₹2.00 per page.** Small jobs under 10,000 pages cost about ₹2.00 per page, 10,000–50,000 pages about ₹1.60, and projects above 50,000 pages about ₹1.20. OCR, detailed indexing, onsite teams and fragile documents add to the base rate.

## What the base rate includes

- Scanning at document-grade resolution in black-and-white, greyscale or colour as agreed
- Image clean-up: de-skew, blank-page removal, orientation correction
- Basic file naming by file, box or batch
- PDF output delivered on a secure drive or by encrypted transfer
- Batch-level quality checks before handover

## What costs extra

- **OCR and searchable PDF:** usually a small add-on per page; makes every word searchable.
- **Metadata indexing:** priced by the number of fields captured per file (for example employee ID, patient UHID, account number, date).
- **Onsite scanning:** scanners, operators and a supervisor deployed at your premises when files cannot leave.
- **Document preparation:** removing staples and pins, repairing torn pages, unbinding registers.
- **Special formats:** bound books, fragile records, large-format drawings and maps.
- **Urgent timelines:** extra scanners and shifts to finish faster.

## What changes your price


The base rate depends on more than the number of pages. Preparation, scanning location, OCR, indexing and delivery format all move the final figure.

### Page volume

Page volume is the biggest cost driver. A project with 5,000 pages and a project with 5 lakh pages need different manpower, scanner capacity, quality checks and delivery planning.

Higher volumes often get better per-page economics because setup costs are spread across more pages.

### Document condition

Scanning clean A4 sheets is different from scanning:

- Stapled files
- Bound registers
- Torn or fragile pages
- Mixed-size documents
- Old carbon copies
- Faded or handwritten records

More preparation means more time before scanning starts.

### Onsite vs offsite scanning

[Onsite document scanning](/onsite-document-scanning-services) may cost more than offsite scanning because scanners, operators, supervisors and workflows must be deployed at your location.

But onsite scanning is often the right choice for confidential records, active files, hospitals, banks, legal teams and government archives.

### OCR and indexing

Basic scanning creates image files. OCR and indexing make files searchable and useful.

Cost changes based on:

- Searchable PDF requirement
- Number of metadata fields
- Manual verification needs
- DMS-ready output
- Excel or CSV index sheets
- Field accuracy expectations

For search-ready files, see [OCR indexing services](/ocr-indexing-services).

### Output format

Common outputs include:

- PDF
- Searchable PDF
- PDF/A
- TIFF, JPEG or PNG
- Excel or CSV metadata
- DMS, EMR, ERP or cloud-ready folders

More structured output usually needs more planning and validation.

### Timeline

Urgent projects may need more scanners, shifts, operators and supervisors. Flexible timelines are usually easier to optimize for cost.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the cost of scanning per page in India?**
Professional bulk scanning costs about ₹1.20 to ₹2.00 per page, depending on volume. Larger projects get a lower per-page rate because setup is spread across more pages.

**Is OCR included in scanning charges?**
Basic scanning produces image PDFs. OCR, which makes the text searchable, is a separate add-on that is quoted per page.

**Do you charge extra for onsite scanning?**
Onsite scanning needs scanners, operators and a supervisor at your location, so it usually costs more than offsite scanning. It is often the right choice for confidential hospital, bank, HR and government records.
