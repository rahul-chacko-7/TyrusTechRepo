HR document scanning services help HR teams move employee records from cupboards, cartons and branch files into secure, searchable digital archives.

For many companies, the biggest issue is not only storage space. HR files are needed during audits, payroll checks, employee verification, compliance reviews, legal queries, exit processing and management discussions. When these records are physical, every request becomes slow.

## HR documents that can be scanned

Typical HR records include:

- Employee personal files
- Joining forms and onboarding documents
- ID proof, address proof and education records
- Payroll documents and salary records
- Attendance, leave and benefits files
- Appraisal and performance records
- Training and policy acknowledgement records
- Transfer, promotion, warning and exit documents
- Statutory compliance documents

## Why indexing matters

Scanning without indexing only creates image files. HR teams need records that can be found by employee ID, employee name, department, location, date, document type or employment status.

Useful HR metadata fields include:

- Employee ID
- Employee name
- Department
- Location or branch
- Joining date
- Document type
- Year
- Active or inactive status

This structure makes the output useful for HRMS, DMS, ERP, cloud folders and internal audit requests.

## Onsite or offsite HR scanning

Sensitive employee files can be scanned onsite when records should not leave the office. Onsite scanning is useful for payroll documents, confidential personnel files, disciplinary records and active employee folders.

Offsite scanning can work well for older HR archives, inactive employee records and large backlogs where logistics and access controls are clearly planned.

Tyrus supports both [onsite document scanning](/onsite-document-scanning-services) and offsite document scanning based on confidentiality, volume and timeline.

## Final thought

The best HR digitization projects start by deciding how files should be searched later. Once the retrieval fields are clear, scanning, OCR, indexing and handover become much easier to control.
