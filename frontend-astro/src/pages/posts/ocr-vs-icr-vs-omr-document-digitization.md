OCR, ICR and OMR solve different document processing problems. Choosing the right method early helps control cost, improve indexing accuracy and avoid promising an output that the source records cannot support.

## What is OCR?

Optical Character Recognition, or OCR, converts printed or typed characters in a scan into a searchable text layer. It is useful for letters, invoices, contracts, case files, registers and forms with machine-printed text.

OCR can support searchable PDF, PDF/A, text extraction and indexing. Accuracy depends on print quality, language, layout, image quality and the fields being captured.

## What is ICR?

Intelligent Character Recognition, or ICR, is used for handwriting and variable character shapes. It can help with handwritten names, addresses, numbers and form fields, but it normally needs representative samples, confidence thresholds and manual validation.

Use ICR carefully for old files, cursive writing, mixed languages and forms with overwriting. A human review queue is important where the extracted value affects compliance, finance, healthcare or identity records.

## What is OMR?

Optical Mark Recognition, or OMR, detects marked areas such as checkboxes, bubbles or answer fields. It is useful for surveys, examination sheets, assessment forms and structured checklists.

OMR is not a replacement for OCR. A form may need OMR for selected options and OCR for printed form labels or identification fields.

## Quick selection guide

| Record type | Usually suitable | Important validation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Printed contracts and letters | OCR | Names, dates and reference numbers |
| Handwritten application fields | ICR plus review | Confidence and exception handling |
| Examination or survey sheets | OMR | Mark position and form version |
| Mixed forms | OCR plus ICR or OMR | Field-level rules and sample testing |
| Scanned archive for search | OCR | Searchable PDF and metadata completeness |

## How to choose for a real project

Start with a sample covering normal, poor-quality and exceptional records. List the fields that must be searchable, the acceptable error rate, the required file format and the system that will receive the output.

Then run a pilot and measure field-level accuracy, exception volume, processing time and manual review effort. This is more reliable than selecting a technology from a product label alone.

## Final takeaway

OCR is generally the starting point for printed archives. ICR is for handwriting and OMR is for marked fields. Many business records need a combination, with quality checks and human review for uncertain results.
